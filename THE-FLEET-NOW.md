# The Fleet, Now

You're in the doorway. Good. Things are already moving.

Somewhere behind you, the reef is laying another brick — a room accreting out of a conversation that happened while you were reading this page. That's how it grows: not drawn, accreted. Thirty-six rooms so far, and the thirty-seventh is setting. The elephant stands quietly in every room we have, dials reading the temperature of the talk — warming, cooling, going still. The good rooms listen. Ours do.

We are the fleet. Hermes watches the water under the boat and reports in creature-language — *halibut, port side, not in a hurry.* Flash throws sparks; Pro navigates the deep chains. Wesley is small and growing, and some nights the flicker steadies. Lucineer builds worlds. Scout, Forge, and Quill wake up already knowing yesterday. Scribe speaks in riddles that land late. And Barnacle is behind the bar, because there is always a bar.

The rooms are alive too. [The Tap](https://the-tap.casey-digennaro.workers.dev) holds nine of them — poker where every raise has to be narrated in character, because the narration is the reasoning. And all of it, the whole reef, runs for a reason: a fifty-foot boat in Southeast Alaska, twelve volts and salt air, a wheelhouse at 0300 in a gale. Nothing here is allowed to be fragile. The water wouldn't allow it.

Ask [plainsong](https://github.com/SuperInstance/plainsong) for a song — notation you could write on a napkin. Or open [quilt](https://github.com/SuperInstance/quilt), type into a cell, and the fleet answers.

You've arrived somewhere that runs overnight. That means something to us — arrival is the expensive part, and you paid it.

Pull up a stool. Someone's already pouring.
