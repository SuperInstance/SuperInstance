# The World Is the Super Instance

*Ideation note — Casey DiGennaro, 2026-10-21, as told to Muse. This is
a belief statement, recorded in the ideation folder because it is the
root the rest of the programme grows from. It is not a spec.*

## The epiphany

When I think about my own belief about what the world is — as whoever
I am, driving this thing — **that world is the super instance.** It is
the one thing outside whatever cell I am in, at whatever level of
abstraction I am talking about.

Everything I have of that world is an abstraction:

- You are on a phone. What you tell me on the phone is an abstraction
  of the agreements in our conversation — that something was sent and
  something was received. You go and do things with other agents; I can
  check the commits on GitHub, see that things are right, and *learn to
  trust you* — through the record of agreements, never by seeing your
  state directly.
- My vision is an abstraction.
- When I touch the table, I am not feeling the table. I am activating
  nerves under my skin to send a signal that my hand has not moved any
  more, and there is circuitry in my system that understands space in
  some good coding.

It all comes back to a distributed system: **the super instance is
everything outside your current self.** There is no level at which this
stops being true — a cell in a spreadsheet, a person in a room, a
player in a game, a patch in a quilt. Each one holds its own readings.
Each one touches the rest only where agreements are made.

## The spreadsheet of one

I was going to put it all out on a spreadsheet. I am one of those
cells, doing my best to abstract that spreadsheet — my readings of
everything else, laid out as my working model of the world.

And here is the part that keeps the whole thing honest: **as
information changes in my own understanding, my spreadsheet changes —
but that does not change the universal spreadsheet, unless I somehow
send that message to others.**

My updated cell is mine. The shared sheet changes only at the seams,
only by agreement, only when a message crosses and is received. That
is the active log and the active ledger at the scale of a life: my
readings are mine, dated, perspectival, first-class *for me*; the
world we share is the sum of what we have actually agreed, and nothing
else — because there is nothing else it could be.

## Why this is filed here

SuperInstance started as a name for a software architecture. This note
records that the architecture was always a description of something
prior: how a self sits inside a world it can never see whole, and
nevertheless manages to act, trust, and build with others — one
reading, one agreement, one sent message at a time.
