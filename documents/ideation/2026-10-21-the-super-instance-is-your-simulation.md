# The Super Instance Is Your Complete Simulation

*Ideation note — Casey DiGennaro, 2026-10-21, as told to Muse. A
companion to "The World Is the Super Instance," filed the same day.
Casey: "that's a thought on its own."*

## The thought

The best way I can say it: **the super instance is your complete
simulation, as you see it.**

I am in my house right now, and I have a simulation going of what my
kid is doing getting ready for soccer over at his mom's house. I have
a simulation of what my brother might be doing in Korea. Simulations
of what different people are doing outside my view, right now. None of
it is seen. All of it is running.

## State carries the vector

I can see birds down at the water, swimming. From the state of the
waves in their little wake behind them I instantly have a story of
the last few seconds — up to a minute. Other birds catching up to the
group, from the same direction, visible in the overall disturbances on
a calm day.

It is not raining, but everything is wet. It *was* raining — and I
would have known it was raining even without remembering it, because
the ground is wet.

**The clues in the state show the vector of motion.** A reading is
never just a snapshot: the present state carries traces of how it got
here, and it is those traces that let eyes, ears and the other senses
extrapolate the immediate past out of the state — and run the
simulation forward from it.

## What this means for the architecture

A dated reading in an active log is not a dead value with a timestamp
bolted on. Read the way a person reads water, the state itself encodes
direction: where this cell has been, how fast, converging or
scattering. The fog-of-war problem — your readings age, the other side
keeps moving — is answered the way it is answered on the water:
**you do not predict from nothing; you read the wake in the current
state and extrapolate the vector.**

The simulation is the log, lived from inside. The ledger is still only
what crosses between us. But the log is richer than a record — it is a
running model of everything outside the self, and its quality is the
quality of the vectors you can read out of the state in front of you.
